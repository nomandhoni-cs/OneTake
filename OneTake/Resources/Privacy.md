# Privacy Policy — OneTake

**Last updated: September 6, 2026**

OneTake is built to work without knowing who you are. There is no account,
no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no backend server of ours.
This policy explains the limited ways the App and its purchase processor
handle information.

## 1. The Short Version

- Your scripts, recordings, and edits stay on your device. We never see,
  upload, or sell them.
- The camera, microphone, and Photos library are used only for the feature
  you invoked, only after you grant permission, and the media never leaves
  your device except when you explicitly save or share it.
- Payments are processed by Apple. Subscription status is managed through
  RevenueCat so the App can unlock Pro and restore it on your other devices.
- We collect no analytics, serve no ads, and perform no cross-app tracking.

## 2. On-Device Access (Permission-Gated)

- **Camera and microphone** — used only while you record in the Studio, to
  capture video and audio into your takes. Accessed only after you grant
  permission (requested in context, when you first record); revocable anytime
  in iPhone Settings, after which recording is unavailable but the rest of
  the App keeps working.
- **Photo library (add-only)** — used only when you save a finished take, to
  write that video to Photos. The App cannot read your library.
- **Scripts, takes, edits, settings** — stored locally on your device in the
  App's private storage. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

## 3. Purchases (Apple + RevenueCat)

Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system and entitlement status is
managed with RevenueCat (our subscription infrastructure provider):

- **Apple** processes your payment and governs billing, trials, renewals,
  cancellations, and refunds under the App Store terms. We never see your
  card or payment credentials.
- **RevenueCat** receives purchase-related information (product identifiers,
  purchase and expiry status, and an anonymous app user identifier plus basic
  device information) strictly to verify your Pro status, unlock features,
  and support restore across your devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy
  (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and Apple's (https://www.apple.com/
  legal/privacy/) for their handling.

## 4. What We Do NOT Collect

No analytics or telemetry of any kind. No advertising identifiers and no
ad tracking (the App never asks for App Tracking Transparency because there
is nothing to track). No location, contacts, or identifiers beyond what §3
requires for purchases. No data is sold or shared for marketing — there is
nothing to sell.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

Local content persists until you delete it (per-take delete, or delete the
App to remove everything). Purchase records live with Apple and RevenueCat
per their retention policies; cancelling Pro does not delete your local
takes. To exercise access/deletion rights over any purchase-related data,
contact us and we will route the request (much of it only Apple or
RevenueCat can action — e.g. receipts and refunds go through Apple).

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, consistent with the 13+
eligibility in our Terms. We knowingly collect no data from children; the
only data flows are the on-device and purchase flows described above.

## 7. Security

Your content is protected by your device (passcode, encryption, sandboxing)
and by OS permission gates. No security is absolute, but there is no server
of ours to breach: the attack surface is your device plus Apple/RevenueCat
infrastructure, each covered by their own protections.

## 8. Changes and Contact

Material changes to this policy bump the in-app legal version and ask for
acceptance again; the current version is always readable in the App under
Profile → Terms & Privacy. Questions or privacy requests: nomandhoni@blinkeye.app.
